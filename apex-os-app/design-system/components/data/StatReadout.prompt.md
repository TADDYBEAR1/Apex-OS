Instrument-cluster stat: a wide-tracked caps label over a large light-weight number.

```jsx
<StatReadout label="Week" value="4" unit="/5" />
<StatReadout label="Total" value={128} accent />
<StatReadout label="Streak" value={6} unit="d" size={32} align="center" />
```

`accent` renders the number in glowing cyan. `size` is the number font-size in px.
