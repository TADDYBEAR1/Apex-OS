The readiness traffic-light verdict from the morning check-in. Gates the day's plan.

```jsx
<ReadinessLight light="green" score={88} recommendation="Full send. Green light on today's plan." />
<ReadinessLight light="yellow" score={64} recommendation="Proceed with caution — cut top sets." />
<ReadinessLight light="red" score={38} recommendation="Swap to recovery work." />
```

States: `green` (GO), `yellow` (CAUTION), `red` (HOLD). The border tints to match.
